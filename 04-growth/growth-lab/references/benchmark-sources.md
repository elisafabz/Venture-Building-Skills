## Search strategy for current benchmarks

When the skill researches benchmarks, use this order:

1. **Your own venture data** (experiments.json, your strategy document)
2. **Market-specific sources** (AdAmigo, Lebesgue, GetResponse for EU)
3. **Global tier-1 sources** (WordStream, Mailchimp, Unbounce)
4. **Quarterly reports** (Skai, Tinuiti)
5. **Fallback tables** (benchmarks.md)

**Always specify in the search:**
- The venture's industry
- The country or region of the target market
- Current year
- Specific channel + metric
