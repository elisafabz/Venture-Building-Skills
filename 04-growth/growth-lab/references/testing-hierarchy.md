# Testing Hierarchy per Channel

The order determines what gets tested next. The top means the highest leverage for the least effort. Do not skip steps.

## Overarching hierarchy

**Audience -> Message -> Channel -> Execution**

First make sure the right audience is being addressed, then test the message, then the channel, then optimise the execution.

## Meta Ads

| Prio | Element | Why first | Ease (1-10) |
|------|---------|-------------|-------------|
| 1 | Hook (first 3 seconds of video / first line of text) | Decides whether anyone keeps watching at all. 80% of the impact. | 10 |
| 2 | Creative format (static vs. video vs. UGC vs. carousel) | Determines engagement level and CPM | 7 |
| 3 | Copy (primary text, headline) | Fine-tuning the message | 9 |
| 4 | CTA (button text, in-text CTA) | Final conversion optimisation | 10 |
| 5 | Audience (Broad vs. Interest vs. Lookalike) | Less important on Meta since 2025+ - "Creative IS the targeting" | 6 |

## Google Ads (Search)

| Prio | Element | Why first | Ease (1-10) |
|------|---------|-------------|-------------|
| 1 | Keywords (match types, negatives, search terms) | Determines who sees the ad at all | 8 |
| 2 | Headlines (RSA, 6-10 unique) | The biggest impact on CTR | 9 |
| 3 | Descriptions (4 unique) | Supports headlines | 9 |
| 4 | Bidding strategy (tCPA, tROAS, Max Conv.) | Efficiency optimisation | 7 |
| 5 | Landing page match | Keyword-ad-LP alignment for Quality Score | 4 |

## Landing Page / Hero Section

| Prio | Element | Why first | Ease (1-10) |
|------|---------|-------------|-------------|
| 1 | Headline (value prop clarity) | Decides whether visitors stay. Uplift up to +104% | 9 |
| 2 | Social proof (testimonials, logos, numbers) | Build trust. Uplift +10-30% | 7 |
| 3 | CTA (text, colour, position, count) | Action trigger. Uplift +20-40% | 9 |
| 4 | Layout (section order, above the fold) | Optimise information flow | 4 |
| 5 | Form (number of fields, multi-step) | Friction reduction. Uplift up to +120% | 8 |

## LinkedIn (Organic + Ads)

| Prio | Element | Why first | Ease (1-10) |
|------|---------|-------------|-------------|
| 1 | Hook (first 2 lines before "see more") | Decides whether anyone clicks | 10 |
| 2 | Format (text, carousel, video, document, poll) | Determines reach and type of engagement | 6 |
| 3 | Content angle (insight, story, contrarian, data) | Resonance with audience | 8 |
| 4 | Hashtags (3-5, niche vs. broad) | Additional reach | 10 |
| 5 | Posting time (Tue-Thu, 8-9 or 17-18 local time) | Marginal impact, but easy to test | 10 |

## Email

| Prio | Element | Why first | Ease (1-10) |
|------|---------|-------------|-------------|
| 1 | Subject line (length, personalisation, emoji) | Decides whether it gets opened | 10 |
| 2 | Preview text (first 90 characters) | Reinforces subject line | 10 |
| 3 | CTA (primary CTA text and placement) | Decides whether it gets clicked | 9 |
| 4 | Send time (day + time) | Influences open rate | 10 |
| 5 | Content structure (length, image vs. text-only) | Engagement + click-through | 5 |
