# Benchmarks - ALWAYS RESEARCH ONLINE

**Ground rule:** there are no universal standards. Benchmarks vary enormously by industry, country, season, audience and venture stage. ALWAYS research them online for the specific venture.

## Mandatory workflow

1. **ALWAYS use WebSearch/Firecrawl** for current benchmarks:
   - Search: "[channel] benchmarks [industry] [country] [year]"
   - e.g. "meta ads benchmarks health [country] 2026"
   - e.g. "landing page conversion rate SaaS B2B 2026"
   - e.g. "email open rate newsletter [country] market 2026"
2. **Cite the source** — where does the benchmark come from?
3. **Account for the venture context** — a startup in week two has different benchmarks from an established product
4. **If web research fails:** use the tables below as an absolute last-resort fallback, but ALWAYS mark them as "fallback benchmark, not verified for this venture"
